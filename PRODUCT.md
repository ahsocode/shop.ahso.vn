# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

AHSO serves business and industrial customers who want to understand the company's capabilities, review industrial solutions or software, and decide whether to request consultation or a quotation. Typical users include factory owners, technical teams, purchasing teams, and decision makers who need a trustworthy partner for industrial systems, production lines, implementation work, or related software.

Internal users are admins and staff who publish solution/software content, maintain showcase materials, and process contact or quotation requests. This site is no longer the primary commerce workflow; product purchasing is handled through the shop subdomain.

## Product Purpose

ahso.vn is the main showcase website for AHSO. It helps visitors quickly understand who AHSO is, what industrial solutions and software the company provides, what completed work or strengths can be reviewed, and how to contact the team for advice or quotation.

The site should make the user's next step obvious: explore a solution, review a software offering, view proof of capability, request a quotation, contact AHSO, or move to the product shop when they specifically need products.

For internal teams, the site should support fast content publishing and clear request handling without carrying unnecessary shop/order-management complexity.

## Brand Personality

AHSO should feel professional, technical, trustworthy, modern, responsive, and premium. The brand voice is expert-led, direct, concise, and practical, with a consultative tone where guidance is useful.

The experience should feel confident and human rather than inflated. It should communicate competence through clarity, real outcomes, structured information, strong visuals, and purposeful motion.

## Anti-references

Avoid interfaces that feel AI-generated, overly colorful, cheap, generic, template-like, visually noisy, or difficult to understand. The site should not overwhelm users with dense blocks of competing information or leave them unsure what to do next.

Avoid the confusing, overloaded browsing experience of sites where too much information appears at once and the user's next action is unclear. Avoid exaggerated claims, sales-heavy language, and decorative effects that reduce trust.

## Design Principles

1. Show capability quickly.
   Visitors should understand AHSO's industrial focus, solution areas, and next actions within a few scrolls.

2. Make expertise easy to scan.
   Use short copy, strong hierarchy, clear section intent, and concise summaries before deeper detail.

3. Build trust through restraint.
   Prefer real proof, project visuals, clear descriptions, and precise language over hype or excessive decoration.

4. Showcase first, then guide action.
   The public site should highlight solutions, software, and outcomes, then guide users toward quotation, consultation, contact, or the shop subdomain.

5. Keep operations practical.
   Admin and staff surfaces should be clear, detailed, fast to use, and focused on publishing content or processing requests.

6. Use polished Vietnamese everywhere.
   All user-facing interface text must use Vietnamese with proper diacritics. English or Vietnamese without diacritics should only appear for unavoidable names, URLs, technical identifiers, or brand terms.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Prioritize readable Vietnamese typography with full diacritics, strong contrast, clear labels, keyboard usability, and obvious feedback for user actions. Motion should be purposeful and moderate, with reduced-motion-friendly behavior where practical. Mobile experiences must be simple, fast, and easy to scan while still preserving enough detail for users to understand solutions and request support.
